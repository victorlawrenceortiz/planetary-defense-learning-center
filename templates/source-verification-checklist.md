# Source Verification Checklist

Use this before sharing planetary-defense or emergency information.

## Check the source

- Is it from NASA/JPL, MPC, IAWN, ESA, NOAA, FEMA/Ready.gov, local emergency management, or another authoritative source?
- Is there a direct source link?
- Is the timestamp current?

## Check the claim

- Is it a close approach, an impact probability, or an official alert?
- Does the source explain uncertainty?
- Are multiple official sources consistent?

## Check the wording

- Is the language calm and factual?
- Does it avoid panic or exaggeration?
- Does it clearly separate education from official emergency instructions?

## If unsure

Do not amplify the claim. Link to official sources and wait for confirmation.
