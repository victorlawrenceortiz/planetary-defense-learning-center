# Go-Bag Checklist

A go-bag supports evacuation or short-term displacement. Adjust for your family, climate, and medical needs.

## Water and food

- Water bottles or water pouches
- Shelf-stable food
- Manual can opener
- Pet food if needed

## Light and power

- Flashlight/headlamp
- Spare batteries
- Phone power bank
- Charging cables

## Health and safety

- First-aid kit
- Prescription medication copies/list
- Masks
- Hand sanitizer
- Whistle

## Documents

- Copies of IDs
- Insurance information
- Emergency contact sheet
- Cash in small bills

## Clothing and comfort

- Weather-appropriate layer
- Gloves/hat
- Small blanket
- Hygiene items

## Review

Check expiration dates and battery charge every 6 months.
