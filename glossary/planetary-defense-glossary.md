# Planetary Defense Glossary

## Planetary defense

The field focused on discovering, tracking, understanding, and potentially mitigating natural impact hazards such as asteroids and comets.

## Near-Earth object / NEO

An asteroid or comet whose orbit brings it near Earth's orbital neighborhood.

## Close approach

A pass near Earth in astronomical terms. A close approach does not automatically mean danger or impact.

## Miss distance

The predicted distance between an object and Earth at closest approach.

## Lunar distance / LD

The average distance between Earth and the Moon, often used to describe close approaches.

## Orbit uncertainty

The range of possible future positions caused by limited or imprecise observations.

## Impact probability

The calculated chance that an object could impact Earth, based on current observations and orbital models.

## Deflection

A method intended to change an object's path enough to avoid impact, usually requiring long warning time.

## Civil preparedness

Practical emergency planning by individuals, families, communities, and agencies.
