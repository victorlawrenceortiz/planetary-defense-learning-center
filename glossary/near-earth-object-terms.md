# Near-Earth Object Terms

## Asteroid

A rocky or metallic small body orbiting the Sun, often from the asteroid belt.

## Comet

An icy body that may form a coma or tail when heated by the Sun.

## Meteoroid

A small natural object in space, smaller than typical asteroids.

## Meteor

The bright streak seen when a meteoroid enters an atmosphere.

## Meteorite

A fragment that survives atmospheric entry and reaches the ground.

## Potentially hazardous asteroid / PHA

An asteroid meeting size and orbit criteria that warrant continued tracking. This label does not mean impact is expected.

## Apparent magnitude

How bright an object appears from Earth.

## Absolute magnitude / H

A standard brightness measure used to help estimate asteroid size.

## Relative velocity

The speed of an object relative to Earth during a close approach.
